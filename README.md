# Analysis of the 2025 Marks & Spencer Ransomware Attack

## About

This repository contains a research paper analysing the 2025 ransomware attack on Marks & Spencer (M&S), one of the UK's major retail companies.

The paper examines the background of the incident, the technical aspects of the cyberattack, its impact on business operations and customers, and the cybersecurity measures that organisations can adopt to reduce the risk and impact of similar attacks.

## Purpose

The purpose of this research is to examine the M&S ransomware incident as a real-world cybersecurity case study and identify important lessons for organisations regarding prevention, incident response, business continuity and cyber resilience.
